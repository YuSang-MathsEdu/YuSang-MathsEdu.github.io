A Mathematics Teacher’s New Age Lament

(printed in the font ‘Euclid’)

A Mindful Journey During a Mathematics Lesson

“Why? Why do I need to take this Mathematics lesson?
What would ‘finding one-third of a segment’ do to my life?”
Fibonacci sequence, Golden ratio, Constructible numbers
Fifth postulate, Angle trisection, Transcendental numbers
Is it a fictional or a mysterious journey?
Or simply a waste of time?

What? What does Mathematics mean to you?
Would a footballer do calculus before striking a football?
Logical thinking and reasoning, Observation, Patterns
Hypothetical conditions and proofs, Language, Discourse
Could it be a metaphorical and a self-discovery journey?
Or an enlightening Element of culture?

How? How many students has this curriculum failed nurturing?
What kinds of skills have we succeeded teaching through Mathematics education?
Critical Thinking, Problem-solving, Communication
Analytical mindset, Creativity, Application
Has anyone experienced such a wonderful journey?
Or even taken the slightest glimpse at such fantasy?

So? What does it take to enjoy such a journey of adventure?
Can we make this Mathematics lesson as fashionable as an award-wining TV series?
Taste of success, Understandable narrative, Motivations
Clear connections, Intrinsic attention, Goals
Do we have the courage to take on such an adventurous journey?
Or shall we try to complete one together?

“Wait! Why do I need to put some efforts to learn Mathematics?
Lest we forget the powerful hands of Artificial Intelligence and LLM!”
Generative AI, Conversational AI, Responsible AI
Emergent Behaviour, Hallucination, Bias
Have we lost our minds before leaving all the decision makings to AI models?
Or shall we earn our Liberty in the old-fashioned way?
